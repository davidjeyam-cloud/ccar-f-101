# Laptop clean-up policy (MOCK - example data for learning)

1. Never delete from a folder marked `safe_to_clear = no` in the folder catalog.
2. A folder marked `review` needs a person to look at the files first.
3. Keep at least 15% of a drive free. Below that, plan a clean-up.
4. Clear `Temp` first, then `Downloads` items older than their keep_days.
5. Rebuildable folders (for example `.venv`) may be deleted to free space.
6. Write down what was cleared, and when, with the save_note tool.
